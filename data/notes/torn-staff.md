- Torn staff (overview): Torn's official staff run the game and enforce the rules. The public roster lives at torn.com/staff.php. As of 2026-11-23 there are 95 staff. Ranks from most to least senior on the game-management side are Admin, Officer, Moderator, and Helper; a separate Developer team builds the game (some on Quality Assurance, Server Administration, or as the Economist). The roster changes over time - never claim someone joined or was promoted 'recently' or is the 'newest' unless a line says so. [source: https://www.torn.com/staff.php | 2026-11-23]
- Staff roles (what they do): Admins run the whole operation. Officers ensure player security and wellbeing, or do script moderation. Moderators moderate user-generated content (forums, profiles, etc.). Helpers assist users and do public moderation; newer Helpers are 'in training'. Developers build and maintain the game. [source: https://www.torn.com/staff.php | 2026-11-23]
- Who created / made / founded Torn: Torn (aka Torn City) was created by Joe Chedburn - known in-game as Chedburn [player ID 1], or just "Ched". He is the game's founder and owner. He began building Torn in 2004 (at age 16, in PHP) and released it on 15 November 2004 under his company Chedburn Networks. So the answer to "who made Torn", "who created Torn", or "who is the founder of Torn" is: Chedburn (Joe Chedburn). [source: https://www.torn.com/staff.php | Torn history | 2026-07-18]
